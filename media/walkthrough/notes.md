# Teach it your business

A database cannot say that status 3 means "shipped" or that amounts are in rupees. Your team can.

Run **DB Explainer: Add a Team Note**, or use the note box under any answer. Notes are saved in `.dbexplainer/notes.md` in your workspace. Commit that file and everyone on the team gets the same, better answers.

Keep notes short and true. DB Explainer includes the notes that match each question.
